Coventry City FC fixtures

Marrickville F5s, Over 45 Men Black. Add the calendar once and every game, time change and field change appears automatically.

Suggested for your device

iPhone, iPad or Mac

Add to Apple Calendar

Tap the button, then Subscribe (iPhone may ask you to confirm first).

Suggested for your device

Google Calendar or Android

Add to Google Calendar

On an Android phone this button may just open the Calendar app without adding anything. If so, open this page on a computer and click the button there, signed in with the same Google account as your phone.

Then, on your phone, turn the calendar on:

  1. Open the Google Calendar app, tap ☰ and then Settings.
  2. Tap Coventry City FC (Marrickville O45 F5s) (under Show more if it's hidden).
  3. Make sure Sync is on.

Outlook

Add to Outlook.com

Anything else

Paste it wherever your calendar app says "subscribe to calendar" or "add from URL". Don't use "import": that copies the games once and never updates.

Google Calendar can take up to a day to show changes; Apple Calendar is quicker.
